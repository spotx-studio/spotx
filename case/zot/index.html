Iterations

Share how I adjust my design to better fit users’ expectation

Iteration 1: From "Over-Flexible" to "Pragmatic Logic"

Backend Complexity
I initially designed a highly customizable module. However, the client's rules were static, and high flexibility increased backend complexity without adding value.
Introduce Independent Customization
We shifted to specific settings. For example, "Funeral Subsidy" categories (Self, 1st/2nd Degree Relative) share a single annual cap, simplifying the backend workload.
Shared Quota Logic
We shifted to specific settings. For example, "Funeral Subsidy" categories (Self, 1st/2nd Degree Relative) share a single annual cap, simplifying the backend workload.

Iteration 2: Streamlining the Approval Workflow

Fragmentation
Originally, a dedicated "Approval Page" provided a comprehensive view but required frequent tab-switching to check application details.
Contextual Sticky Action
I replaced the separate page with a sticky action bar. Reviewers can scroll through details and approve instantly without leaving the context, significantly boosting efficiency.

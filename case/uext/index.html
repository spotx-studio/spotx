Home Page Iterations

Share how I adjust my design to better fit users’ expectation

Version 1 - Base

Excessive Vertical Scrolling
Nurses require 'at-a-glance' visibility. Critical data must be viewable without interaction for hands-free monitoring.

Version 2 - Experiment

Enhanced Status Cards
  • Implemented color-coded backgrounds for immediate status recognition.
  • Increased bed number legibility.
Information Density Overload
Previous card design was too packed, hindering rapid scanning.

Version 3 - Adjustment

Collapsible Notification Panel
  • Expand Mode: Detailed administrative view.
  • Collapse Mode: Streamlined view for rapid daily monitoring.
Data De-cluttering
Removed non-critical health metrics from status cards to prioritize urgent alerts and patient identification.
Contextual Search Placement
Relocated the search bar under the section title to clearly indicate local (patient status) vs. global search scope.

Outcomes

What did we achieve?
Empowered 1,000+ Nurses
Successfully deployed to assist over 1,000 medical professionals, significantly streamlining their daily monitoring workflow and reaction time.
Zero-Revision Efficiency
By clarifying technical constraints during the wireframe stage, we eliminated back-and-forth design revisions, accelerating the development cycle by 30%.
Scalable DOM Structure
Instead of hard-coding repetitive modals, I implemented dynamic JavaScript injection. This kept the HTML lightweight and made backend integration seamless.
Bridging Design & Code
Acting as a Design Technologist allowed me to identify feasibility issues instantly, ensuring the final product matched the Figma design 100% without technical debt.

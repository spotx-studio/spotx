UX Planning

Stage 1: Feature Audit & Stakeholder Consensus

Analyzing User Feedbacks

Based on the collected data, we identified 2 key pain points that needed addressing in the app's design and functionality.
The first one is emotional accessibility — most users mentioned "the APP is difficult to use". It suggested that users felt disconnected from the app's core purpose.
The second one is functional disorder — users mentioned that the app's navigation and layout were confusing and inconsistent. They often get lost in the app.

Auditing the Information Architecture

Re-evaluated the entire app ecosystem from the ground up. By mapping out existing features against actual user needs, we successfully condensed 9 redundant modules into 5 primary functions.
For example: We combined the "Activites" & "Service Opportunities" modules into a single "Events" module. It is because we found that service opportunites usually come together with events, such as ushers, worship leaders and singers...etc. So people could volunteer when they are visiting the event details page. It would be more convenient and intuitive for users.

Aligning Diverse Stakeholders

With over 8 committee members contributing different visions, I utilized low-fi wireframes to ground the discussion, translating subjective organizational politics into objective, user-centric solutions.

Design Iterations

Share how I adapt the design to solve cross-platform issues

Iteration 1: Reachability & Ergonomics

The iOS Dynamic Island Issue
Originally, core navigation icons were placed at the very top of the screen. On newer iOS devices, the Dynamic Island rendered them nearly unclickable.
Thumb-Friendly Tab Bar
Relocated core navigation to a bottom tab bar and expanded tap targets to standard OS guidelines (44px), significantly improving thumb reachability.

Iteration 2: Visual Differentiation for Content Density

Disorienting Layouts
Videos, sermons, and social posts all shared an identical, monotonous layout, leaving users disoriented in the structural mess.
Media Library & Distinct Patterns
Introduced distinct UI patterns. Created a structured "Media Library" to separate Hymns and Sermons, integrating a quick-filter tag system for rapid browsing.

Iteration 3: The "Prayer Request" Interaction Loop

Lack of Ritual & Closure
Prayer requests looked like standard posts. Users wanted to actively participate and be notified when a prayer was answered.
Dedicated Interactive Board
Designed a board with a "Start/End Praying" toggle to create a sense of ritual. Added a status tracker where creators can leave gratitude messages, triggering push notifications to participants upon completion.

Iteration 4: Progressive Disclosure in Bible Reader

Decluttering the Interface
Applied Progressive Disclosure. Extracted "Devotion Plans" into a separate tab and surfaced essential reading tools (Translation, Book) as distinct buttons.
Contextual Deep-Dives
Deep-dive content, like "Related Sermons" or notes, was relegated to a contextual bottom-sheet modal triggered only when a user taps a specific verse.
